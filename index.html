Campaign rules

Supply points
Update your SP balance by hand when the group gains or spends supplies.
Damage
Update remaining armor and structure after a session. Filled pips show intact points. Outlined pips show lost points.
Critical hits
Use the plus and minus buttons in the unit editor to record hits and repairs. Each filled pip marks one hit. Apply its listed effect at the table.
Inoperable units
Tick Inoperable when a unit cannot deploy. Clear it when the unit can return to service.
