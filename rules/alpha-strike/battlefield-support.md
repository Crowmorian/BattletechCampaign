# Battlefield Support — Quick Reference

Battlefield Support cards represent artillery barrages and aerospace strikes without deploying additional miniatures.

### Before the Battle

- Players receive a Battlefield Support Points (**BSP**) budget determined by the scenario.
- Recommended introductory budget: **5 BSP per player**.
- Select support cards secretly before the game.
- Each purchased card can be used **once**, even if its attack misses.
- BSP is separate from normal unit PV.

### Using a Support Card

1. **Declare:** During the Combat Phase, reveal the support card.
2. **Target:** Select an enemy 'Mech or a Point of Impact, depending on the support type.
3. **Roll:** Roll 2D6 against the card's Target Number.
4. **Resolve:** Apply damage or scatter if applicable.
5. **Discard:** The support card is spent regardless of success.

Support attacks use their printed Target Numbers without normal attack modifiers.

### Direct Aerospace Strikes

- Target a single enemy 'Mech.
- On success, deal the listed damage.
- On failure, the attack misses completely.

### Artillery & Bombing (AoE)

- Select a Point of Impact (POI).
- On success, center the AoE template on that location.
- Every 'Mech whose base touches the template takes the listed damage.
- On failure, the attack scatters.

### Scatter Procedure

1. Roll 1D6 for the scatter direction using the numbered AoE template.
2. Roll another 1D6 for distance.
3. Multiply the distance result by **2 inches**.
4. Move the impact point and resolve AoE damage at the new location.

**Example:** An artillery attack misses. The direction roll is 3 and the distance roll is 4. The impact scatters 8" in direction 3.

### Important Rules

- Terrain, elevation and buildings do not reduce Battlefield Support damage under the box-set rules.
- No artillery or aircraft miniature is required.
- An AoE strike can hit multiple units.
- Missed artillery and bombing attacks can still cause damage after scattering.

**Remember:** Support cards provide additional tactical options, but every use is a limited resource. Choose your targets carefully!
