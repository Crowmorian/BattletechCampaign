# Jumping & Elevation — Quick Reference

### Jump Distance

- A unit's Jump Move (`6"j`, for example) determines its maximum horizontal jump distance.
- Vertical elevation changes do **not** reduce horizontal jump distance.
- Jumping ignores intervening terrain movement costs.
- Measure the shortest horizontal distance between takeoff and landing.

### Maximum Jump Height

**Maximum height = Starting elevation + (Jump Move ÷ 2, rounded down)**

| Jump Move | Maximum Rise |
| --------- | ------------ |
| 4"j       | 2"           |
| 6"j       | 3"           |
| 8"j       | 4"           |
| 10"j      | 5"           |
| 12"j      | 6"           |

**Example:** A 'Mech with `6"j` standing on a 2" plateau can clear terrain reaching 5" above the tabletop (2" starting elevation + 3" jump height).

The landing point must also be within the allowed jump distance.

### Jumping Combat Modifiers

- **Attacking after jumping:** +2 to attack Target Number.
- **Being attacked after jumping:** +1 to normal TMM.
- **Special jump abilities:** Apply any relevant JMPW/JMPS modifiers.

### Important Restrictions

- Jumping into water is allowed; jumping out of water is normally prohibited.
- Jumping units may land facing any direction.
- Jumping does not permit landing in prohibited or occupied locations.
- Ordinary ground movement pays additional elevation movement costs; jumping ignores them.

**Remember:** Jump distance and jump height are separate limits. A 'Mech must satisfy both to complete a jump.
