# 1. Building Your Company

- **Starting roster:** 6 BattleMechs, maximum **200 PV** combined.
- **Pilot Skill:** All pilots begin at Skill 4.
- **Mission deployment:** Up to 4 'Mechs, maximum **100 PV** combined.
- You may deploy fewer than 4 'Mechs if you prefer heavier, more expensive machines.
- Undeployed units remain safely in reserve.

Your company can grow through future contracts, purchases and battlefield salvage.
