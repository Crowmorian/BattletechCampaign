# 4. Battlefield Salvage

Destroyed BattleMechs may be recovered if your forces **control the battlefield when the engagement ends**.

Roll 2D6 for each recoverable wreck:

| 2D6   | Result                                        |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 2–4   | Total loss — beyond recovery                  |
| 5–7   | Heavy salvage — extensive rebuilding required |
| 8–10  | Recoverable — normal rebuilding costs         |
| 11–12 | Excellent salvage — 25% repair discount       |

- Salvage must be secured and assigned to a company.
- Recovered machines are initially non-operational.
- Salvaged units may join your roster once rebuilt.
- Retreating from a battlefield means abandoning any wrecks you cannot recover.
