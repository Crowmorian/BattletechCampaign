# 2. Damage & Repairs

Damage persists between missions. Record lost armor, structure damage and critical hits on your roster.

| Repair            | Cost |
| ----------------- | ---- |
| 1 Armor point     | 1 SP |
| 1 Structure point | 3 SP |
| 1 Critical hit    | 2 SP |

- Repairs are optional. You can save your funds or repair only part of the damage.
- Repairs are completed between missions once paid for.
- Routine maintenance and ammunition are free.
- Destroyed units cannot be repaired normally; they must first be recovered and rebuilt.
